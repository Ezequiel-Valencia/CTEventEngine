Remix @ The Well

DJ and drinks with a side of company

November 11, 2026 | 6pm–8pm |
  • Download ICS file
  • Add to Google Calendar
  • Add to Office 365 / Outlook.com
The Well

Instructions

The Well is located in the lower level of Yale Schwarzman Center. Enter the building at 168 Grove Street and take the stairs or elevator one floor down. 

WANT TO ATTEND? 

  • Free and open to the public ages 21+ with valid government ID.
  • No registration required. 
  • SIGN UP and let us know you plan to attend. 

Tags

Art Access, Collaboration, Music

Remix @ The Well invites Yale students to showcase their talents as a DJ for a night at The Well, our onsite pub. 

For tonight's edition, schumi, aka Athena Schumacher MA '27, will spin house, commercial/pop, R&B, and international tracks (French, Spanish, and more)!

Enjoy the vibes with light bites and beverages from The Well and you can also order burgers on Wednesdays!
