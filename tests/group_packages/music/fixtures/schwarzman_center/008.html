Tags

Classes, Demonstrations & Workshops, Dance

EveryBody Dances @ Yale Schwarzman Center brings local and visiting dance artists to our Dance Studio to teach masterclasses in jazz, hip hop, salsa, modern, contemporary, precision dance and more! All community members are welcome. This week's class is Performance as Ritual led by Thuthuka Sibisi.

Join multidisciplinary artist Thuthuka Sibisi for an introductory workshop on the ritual of solo dance creation and performance. Sibisi, who is accomplished in both music and physical theater and movement, will share foundational elements of his creative practice, offering a gateway into a process that requires both spiritual and physical rigor. Participants will be guided through a series of movement explorations, reflective practices, and embodied rituals to deepen their connection to their craft and begin experimenting with their own ritual-based approaches to creation and performance. Takeaways will benefit artists, performers, and creatives of all disciplines and styles.
