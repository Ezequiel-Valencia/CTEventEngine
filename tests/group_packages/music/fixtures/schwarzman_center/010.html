Pink Martini All-Stars Present: A Season of Stars With Edna Vazquez, Jimmie Herrod, and Ari Shapiro

December 11, 2026 | 7:30pm–11:59pm |

Instructions

Commons is located at Yale Schwarzman Center, 168 Grove Street, New Haven, CT 06511.

  • This event is free and open to the public.
  • Yale advance REGISTRATION opens Friday, Nov 13 at 10am
  • Public REGISTRATION opens Friday, Nov 13 at 4pm

If you need technical assistance with registration or require accessible accommodations contact ysc.info@yale.edu.

Thomas Lauderdale’s globe-trotting “little orchestra” Pink Martini is back with a dazzling holiday show that brings triple the joy! Featuring three of the band’s favorite singers and longtime collaborators, the Pink Martini All-Stars present A Season of Stars, which conjures up a mix of old holiday favorites and sweet new treats, glittering with the band’s unique brand of catchy classic pop, big band sound, and Hollywood glamour. The illustrious Ari Shapiro, Yale alumnus from the Class of 2000 and celebrated former NPR host, joins the soulful Edna Vazquez and the sensational America’s Got Talent finalist Jimmie Herrod to lead the way, their three vibrant voices decking the halls on a spirited global holiday journey. This special evening is destined to bring three times the sparkle to the festive season, along with a whole lot of dancing fun.
