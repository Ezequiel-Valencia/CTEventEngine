Event Details

GREASE is the word this fall! Bringing energy, humor, and unforgettable music  to the stage, this trip to Rydell High in the 1950's follows Danny, Sandy, the Pink Ladies, and the T-Birds as they navigate friendship, romance, and the pressures of being a teenager. Featuring iconic songs including "Summer Nights," "Greased Lightning" and "You're the One That I Want," GREASE will bring magic changes to audiences of all ages.

The show will be performed by students of Sacred Heart Academy, with male actors from 12 local high schools, in celebration of Sacred Heart Academy's 80th Anniversary!

Holiday Food and Toy Drive

Please consider bringing donations of non-perishable food items and new, unwrapped toys when you attend events at the theatre through the holidays. Donations will be distributed to New Haven families in need.
