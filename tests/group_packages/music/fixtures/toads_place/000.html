Our Story

Toad’s Place is Connecticut’s premier night club, featuring live music of all genres and college dance parties.

Toad’s Place has been hoppin’ since 1975, establishing itself as one of the premier music halls in the country. Virtually every major touring act has appeared on Toad’s stage in the past fifty years. From The Rolling Stones to Post Malone, Johnny Cash to Dan + Shay, Bob Dylan to Snoop Dogg: Toad’s Place hasn’t skipped a beat in decades.
